2025 年 University Physics Competition（UPC）题目与官方信息

赛事概况

In this year’s competition 838 teams submitted papers for judging.

Problem A

Acoustic Preservation of the Electric Sasando The sasando is a traditional Indonesian string instrument where sound is produced by vibrating strings acoustically amplified through a hand-formed, dried lontar leaf resonator. This unique resonator naturally amplifies frequencies between 98 Hz and 1047 Hz. The strings are mounted on a bamboo tube measuring 70 cm in length with a diameter of 8 cm. Modern electric versions replace the resonator with a pickup and speaker, sacrificing acoustic quality for portability. Your challenge is to develop a method for the electric sasando to replicate the authentic acoustic sound of the traditional instrument.

Problem B

Artillery Consider a cannon 150 or 200 years ago that fired spherical 5 kg projectiles that are 11cm in diameter with a muzzle velocity of up to 450 m/s. If our target is at a horizontal distance of 1,200 meters, and both the cannon and the target are at an altitude of 500 meters above sea level, what muzzle velocity and firing angle above the horizontal will successfully hit the target? Then, consider targets at horizontal distances between 1,000 meters and 1,500 meters, at a variety of different altitudes, and accounting for different amounts of wind. Develop a method that an artillery officer trained in mathematics, but lacking computers or calculators, could use to quickly estimate a successful muzzle velocity and firing angle.
