写作句库 —— 从 73 篇金牌论文中摘录的英文句式

用法：按论文部位查找，直接套用后替换占位内容。每句注明出处与使用场景。这些句子的共同点：信息密度高、无废话、自带逻辑连接词——评委扫读时靠它们抓住你的思路。

1. 摘要（Abstract）

句式 出处 场景
"We define the radius of Neptune, R_N, as the distance from the center of mass at which pressure is equal to 1 bar; that is, R_N ≈ 24764 km." 2010-A-160 摘要第二句就钉死符号与数值，全文无歧义
"If the drag force is too strong, ... On the other hand, if the drag force is too weak, ..." 2010-A-160 两句话立起问题的核心 trade-off
"We conclude that 8.8296° < θ < 8.8549°, yielding an allowed margin of error of 0.0253°." 2010-A-160 摘要直接给数值结论（带区间与精度）
"In this solution, we investigate ... We conclude that the Magnus force is the main reason that drives ..." 2015-B-108 摘要首句点题、次句给主因结论

2. 基准与动机（Baseline / Motivation）

句式 场景
"How much fuel would be required to ... without X?" 用设问开启对照组章节
"This anchors the comparison for Section 2." 明说基准的用途
"If X does not fire its boosters, it will continue traveling in a hyperbolic orbit." 推演"不作为"的后果，制造建模必要性
"The purpose of the trajectory we designed is to ...; we can therefore require ..." 由设计目的自然引出判据

3. 建模与假设（Model / Assumptions）

句式 出处 场景
"Define σ ≡ c_d M_H2 A / 2k_B." 2010-A-160 组合参数：把常数打包，方程从此干净
"Fixing these points, we approximate the dependence as P(h) = αe^(−h/β)." 2010-A-160 两点锚定拟合的标准表述
"To simplify our calculations, we assume the atmosphere is composed entirely of H₂." 承认简化但理由充分
"We choose this value as it is comparable to the size of Voyager 2 [7]." 给参数找现实锚点（同类比飞船/仪器）
"Since the purpose is to ..., a 'significant' saving is not quantitative enough. For a fair estimation we decided to set 20%." 2010-A-160 操作化模糊词并自辩（全文最值得抄的一句）
"Without loss of generality, we assume the velocity has a non-negative component in the positive x direction." 对称性消冗余

4. 数值方法（Numerics）

句式 场景
"The equations of motion were numerically integrated using Euler's method. Specifically, time was discretely incremented in steps of Δt = 0.001 s." 方法名+步长，一句话
"The code is composed so that, when ..., both drag and gravity act; whereas when ..., only gravity has an effect." 把代码分支讲成物理事件
"Note that the spacecraft may leave the outer atmosphere into space or into Neptune." 预告边界情形
"Halving the step ... changes the exit speed in the fifth digit; we therefore claim 4–5 significant figures, worst case 4." 步长减半收敛声明的完整版
"Because the uncertainty is greatest in this critical case, we conclude a precision of ... throughout the simulation." 用最坏情形给全程精度封顶

5. 结果与论证（Results / Reasoning）

句式 场景
"We show below that exit velocity is directly proportional to exit angle." 单调性先行，夹逼合法
"Figure 1 shows a trajectory with θ = 8.8295° and v_e > v_max. On the other hand, Figure 2 shows ... We conclude then that 8.8295° < θ_min < 8.8296°." 反例夹逼的叙事模板
"As we will see, v_c > v_min; consequently, θ_max does not correspond to v_min but instead to v_c." 诚实处理推理中的意外转折
"This can be compared with our estimate from Section 1 of ..." 强制回扣基准
"The decrease in velocity is less than 0.5%." 极端情形的量化描述

6. 不确定性与局限（Uncertainty / Limitations）

句式 场景
"There is an inevitable uncertainty involved due to limited precision of the computer numerical simulations." 开门见山承认数值误差
"We also need to note that there are many other factors ... we haven't taken into account. For example, ..." 未建模因素清单（限速/自转/风）
"These approximations can be improved in a more detailed analysis." 一句话给局限兜底，不展开

7. 结论（Conclusion）

句式 出处 场景
"As Δθ is so small, we would advise against relying on ..." 2010-A-160 负结论：敢说"不建议"
"Our analysis can be extended to consider ... This will produce a much larger allowed margin of error." 指出放宽条件的出路
"We should mention that we have assumed ..." 结论区补显式假设声明
"We extend our sincerest congratulations..."（官网语）——不要学这句，它属于主办方 反例

8. 高频逻辑连接词（让段落"自己会走路"）

  • 递进补充：Specifically / In particular / That is（即）/
  • 因果链：Consequently / Therefore / As a result / This implies
  • 对让步：However / On the other hand / Although / Whereas / Despite
  • 限定边界：provided that / in the range between X and Y / to leading order
  • 引导结论：We conclude that / We find that / This yields / which gives

9. 反面清单（这些写法在落选论文里高发）

  1. ❌ "We use a very accurate model..." —— 自夸无证据；改为给出精度声明。
  2. ❌ "Obviously, ..." —— 评委不觉得 obvious；改为一步推导或引用式号。
  3. ❌ 摘要只有"We analyzed the problem and got good results."——没有数字的摘要在 UPC 等于没写。
  4. ❌ "It can easily be shown that..." 跳过关键推导——金牌论文连火箭方程都全文重推。
  5. ❌ 图无编号引用（"as shown below"）——每张图必须 "Figure N" 且被正文点名。
