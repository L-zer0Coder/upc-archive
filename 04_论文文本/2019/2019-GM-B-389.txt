Abstract
This paper analyses the track and motion of a roller coaster.Our main task can be
divided into three parts:
(1)Design a roller coaster that meets both security and stimulation.
We make the following analysis: While riding a roller coaster, however, being
overweight or weightless and changing direction can cause changes in acceleration,
resulting in a sense of excitement. However, excessive acceleration is bound to cause
damage to the health of passengers. Normal adults can withstand 4~5g vertical
acceleration with a duration of no more than 0.1s.According to the theoretical
calculation, the maximum vertical acceleration in this design scheme is 4.1g, and the
duration is 0.01s, which occurs at the first downhill. The rest of the vertical
acceleration is less than 4g. The time of overweight and weightlessness accounts for
87% of the total time. Therefore, this design scheme can continuously bring
passengers a sense of tension and stimulation under the premise of ensuring safety.
(2)Analyze the dynamics of roller coaster and calculate the total duration.
The roller coaster track is divided into 9 segments. In each segment, we use
Kinetic energy theorem to analyse its motion, and then obtain velocities between
every two adjacent segments. These velocities correspond well with results of
simulation. With the help of simulation softwares, we figure out the total duration of
the ride is 1 minute and 2.67seconds.
(3)Accurately describe the motion trajectory of roller coaster in three
dimensions.
According to (1) and (2), we can precisely describe the path that the roller
coaster track would follow.
Keywords:
Roller coaster, Simulation, Kinetic energy theorem, Dynamical
analysis

1. Problem Analysis
1.1Introduction
Roller Coaster is an exciting activity, which brings riders a lot of
fun, excitement and thrill. Aboard on a roller coaster, the sense of
weightless and overweight when the roller coaster up and down is
why we feel excited of thrilled. However, there are some accidents
causing injuries or deaths, a few of which may result from some
unreasonable design. What’s more, the up-and-down roller coaster
could possibly make us feel sick if the sense of acceleration is too
excessive. In this paper, we try to analyse how a roller coaster works
and try to design a roller coaster safe enough as well as exciting. We
design different parts of its path, including riser, vertical ring, arc
segments and so on. The dynamic analysis of each part will be posted
in this paper.
1.2Problem Analysis
The roller coaster is required to start from a horizontal section of
20 meters and rise to the highest point of a mountain under the action
of external forces. The height of the highest point is 30 meters, and its
gravitational potential energy is the only energy source of the roller
coaster motion. The roller coaster track should be closed and not

Firstly, we start with roller coaster track design. Through
consulting materials, we know that the track of roller coaster is usually
combination of a series of mechanical models, which make the car body
climb, fall, reverse and other actions along the track. Track systems
should be considered for safety and stimulation. We make the following
analysis: roller coaster stimulation mainly depends on the acceleration,
through the appropriate overweight, weightlessness, impact, vibration
and other sensory stimulation to the passengers. However, excessive
acceleration will cause harm to human body, if affect vestibular organ,
make human body produces the undesirable physiology reaction such as
dizziness, vomiting. Accelerated passage can also cause stress on the
heart, blood pressure, etc.
Therefore, we must come up with a feasible plan to keep the
roller coaster's acceleration within a certain range, which is both
